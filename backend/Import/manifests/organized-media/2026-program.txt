The Kanun of the Farm
Kosovo
Prod.:Urban Theater
Director: Xheraldina Krasniqi
Cast: Armend Zeqiri, Armend Ballazhi, Agnes Nokshiqi, Minka Berisha, Dren Arifi & Argjend Bashota
20:00
Synopsis:The Kanun of the Farm is a contemporary theatrical adaptation inspired by Animal Farm. Through symbolic and satirical language, the performance explores mechanisms of power, manipulation, and the transformation of ideals within a society that initially strives for equality but gradually reproduces the same oppressive structures it once opposed. Combining political allegory with physical ensemble work, the production reflects on collective obedience, corruption, and the fragile boundaries between revolution and tyranny.

Chicken
North Macedonia
Prod.:Vortex Theater / Skopje Comedy Theater
Director: Teodora Siljanoska
Cast: Tome Stankovski, Biljana Dragikevik Projkovska, Ane Blazovski, Vanesa Trimcevska & Kristina Gieva
20:00
Synopsis:Chicken is a contemporary ensemble performance exploring fear, conformity, and the longing for freedom in a society shaped by routine, manipulation, and emotional emptiness. 20:00
